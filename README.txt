CHERS WEBSITE – CUSTOMER EXPERIENCE UPDATE

WHAT'S INCLUDED
- Responsive white and rouge-pink storefront.
- Shop filters: product type, wedding role, colour, budget tier, style and search. No material filter.
- Product-level colour/style/size/budget preference and optional product note.
- Cart quantities, remove item, saved cart using this browser's local storage.
- Wishlist saved in this browser.
- WhatsApp enquiry checkout with delivery location, PIN code, wedding date, event date, customer name, phone, theme colours, video-call request and notes.
- Inspiration-photo picker with clear instruction to attach photos manually in WhatsApp (websites cannot attach local files to a WhatsApp message link).
- Wedding collection builder pathways and Instagram portfolio links.

PUBLISH ON GITHUB PAGES
1. Extract this ZIP.
2. Open the extracted Chers_Website_Generated folder.
3. Upload the CONTENTS of that folder into the root of your existing GitHub repository (not the enclosing folder).
4. Replace the previous index.html, styles.css, script.js and README.txt, and upload/merge the images folder without deleting existing images.
5. Commit changes. GitHub Pages should redeploy automatically.

IMPORTANT LIMITATIONS / BEFORE GOING LIVE
- The current catalogue still contains four featured product listings and six uploaded brand/product images. Add the full SKU catalogue and genuine product photos before presenting this as a complete catalogue.
- Product prices are not displayed; pricing is confirmed by Chers over WhatsApp.
- WhatsApp number in the site is 919380431760. Verify it before publishing.
- Cart and wishlist are stored locally in the customer's browser; they are not synced across devices and are not visible to Chers until the customer sends the WhatsApp enquiry.
- Checkout is an enquiry, not payment processing or a confirmed order.
- There is no live inventory, booking-slot calendar, order tracking, customer account, or backend/admin dashboard. These require a database/backend or commerce platform.
- Inspiration images selected in the form are not uploaded automatically; customer attaches them after WhatsApp opens.
- Confirm store address, opening hours, policies, shipping timelines and any customer testimonials before adding them publicly. No reviews have been invented.
